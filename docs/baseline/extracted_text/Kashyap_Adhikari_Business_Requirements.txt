1. Executive Summary
The platform will preserve and responsibly grow a nationwide, verified genealogy of Kashyap/Kashyapa Gotra Adhikari families while providing member profiles, culturally governed relationship identification, events, invitations, notifications, community publishing, household mapping, cultural knowledge and secure communication. The trusted genealogy is the shared foundation for every dependent service.
Release decisionAll approved capabilities in this BRD are mandatory for the first public market release. Internal delivery may use milestones, feature flags and controlled pilots, but no listed capability is deferred merely as a future phase.

1.1 Business problem
Genealogy knowledge is fragmented across paper records, individual custodians and disconnected family branches.
Unverified edits, duplicate identities and unclear relationship paths can damage community trust.
Cultural knowledge and event information are difficult to maintain, validate and distribute consistently.
Members lack one privacy-aware platform for discovery, invitations, announcements, support and communication.
1.2 Intended outcomes
One auditable source of truth for verified genealogy.
Faster, governed member contribution without direct alteration of official records.
Accurate relationship and cultural services based only on approved rule sets.
Safe bilingual participation across Nepal and the diaspora.
Sustainable administration, moderation, support, backup and recovery.

3.2 Explicit scope decisions

Genealogy person records persist independently when an account is deleted, subject to correction, restriction and lawful governance.
No payment, e-commerce or advertising billing capability is included unless approved through formal change control.
The product supports Nepal-wide families and invited diaspora members; the primary legal and cultural operating context is Nepal.

4.1 Segregation of duties
A requester cannot approve the same genealogy request.
A content author cannot provide the final religious/cultural approval for their own authoritative rule.
Production data access requires named accounts; shared administrator credentials are prohibited.
Privileged actions are logged and reviewed. Emergency access is time-limited and retrospectively approved.
Duplicate merging, rollback and bulk export require elevated authorization and secondary confirmation.

10. Privacy, Safety and Legal Requirements
The solution shall undergo Nepal-qualified legal review before production. The design baseline considers the Privacy Act, 2075; Individual Privacy Regulation, 2077; Electronic Transactions Act, 2063; and Act Relating to Children, 2075. This BRD records product controls, not legal advice.
Use clear purpose notices and obtain versioned consent before collecting optional sensitive data.
Collect only fields necessary for defined purposes; prohibit secondary use without authorization and notice.
Provide access, correction, restriction, account closure and complaint channels.
Use privacy-by-default for living people, minors, phone numbers, addresses and exact locations.
Prohibit sale of member data and behavioral advertising based on genealogy or cultural participation.
Conduct privacy impact and threat assessments before launch and for material changes.
Publish Terms of Use, Privacy Notice, Community Standards, Child Safety, Moderation and Retention notices in Nepali and English.
Define breach triage, containment, notification assessment, evidence preservation and post-incident review.
10.1 Child and vulnerable-person safeguards
Age declaration and guardian workflow where applicable.
No public exact location, school/contact information or open messaging for minors by default.
Guardian ability to review permitted visibility and account relationships.
Rapid reporting/escalation for exploitation, threats, doxxing or self-harm concerns.
Safety decisions override engagement objectives.

12. Security Requirements
Threat model identity, genealogy, content, messaging, media, maps, notifications, admin and integrations.
OTP rate limits, anti-enumeration, bot protection and suspicious-login controls.
Role- and attribute-based authorization with branch scope and deny-by-default policy.
Privileged MFA, step-up authentication, session timeout and periodic access review.
Secrets stored outside source code; key rotation and environment separation.
Input validation, output encoding, parameterized queries and malware/file-type scanning.
Signed or short-lived media access; prevent insecure direct object references.
Tamper-evident audit logs for sensitive reads, writes, exports, role changes, impersonation and recovery.
Security testing includes SAST, dependency/container scanning, DAST and independent penetration testing.
Document incident severity, on-call ownership, containment, recovery and post-incident actions.
