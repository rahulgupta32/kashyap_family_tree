1. Purpose, authority and use
This specification converts the frozen product baseline into the mandatory operating model for design, engineering, testing, data migration, deployment and handover. It supplements rather than replaces the Master Specification v1.1. If a conflict occurs, the Documentation Baseline & Freeze Record v1.0 and approved change records determine precedence.
Every epic, story, design, API, database migration, test and release must trace to approved requirement identifiers.
The agency may propose implementation choices but may not change product scope, cultural authority, privacy controls, acceptance criteria or Jyphra ownership.
Pending cultural, legal and named-authority decisions are implemented behind controlled configuration or neutral fallback until approved.
Production deployment is prohibited until all applicable release gates have objective evidence.

10. Definition of Ready
Requirement and source IDs are identified; business value and user/role are clear.
Acceptance criteria include success, validation, authorization, privacy, error, audit and localization behaviour.
Dependencies, domain approvals and unknowns are listed; unresolved items have safe configuration/fallback.
UX states cover loading, empty, error, offline/retry, denied, pending and success where applicable.
API/data changes, migration impact, analytics and observability are defined.
Security/privacy classification and abuse cases are reviewed.
Test approach and test data are feasible; story is sized and fits the sprint.
11. Definition of Done
Code is reviewed by an independent qualified reviewer and all mandatory CI checks pass.
Acceptance criteria pass on supported platforms; regression, negative and permission tests are included.
No secrets, sensitive data or prohibited logs are introduced; dependency and static scans meet policy.
API/schema documentation, ADRs, localization keys, analytics and operational notes are updated.
Accessibility checks and Nepali/English content checks pass for affected interfaces.
Database changes are backward compatible where required, rehearsed and rollback-capable.
Feature flags/default configuration are safe; monitoring and alerts exist for material failure modes.
Product Owner accepts the story and RTM links requirement, implementation and evidence.

13. Branching, review and versioning
Short-lived feature branches from protected main; no direct production commits.
Pull requests identify requirement/story IDs, risk, testing, screenshots where relevant, migration and rollback impact.
At least one independent review; two approvals for security-, privacy-, infrastructure- or irreversible-data changes.
Conventional, traceable commits; signed release tags and immutable build artifacts.
Semantic versioning for public APIs/apps; database migrations and rule catalogues have independent ordered versions.
Hotfixes follow expedited review but never bypass test evidence, audit or post-incident review.

16. Architecture delivery controls
Use modular boundaries for identity/profile, genealogy, cultural rules, content/community, communication, notifications, administration and audit.
Use a transactional system of record for authoritative data and purpose-built indexes/caches for discovery; indexes are rebuildable, not authoritative.
APIs are versioned and contract-first; idempotency is required for retryable writes and imports.
Asynchronous work uses durable queues, retry limits, dead-letter handling and correlation IDs.
Genealogy graph operations preserve provenance, effective state, disputes and immutable change history.
Cultural calculations are versioned by approved rule set and return explanation/provenance; unapproved rules never masquerade as authoritative.
Critical managed services are isolated across availability zones where the selected scenario supports it; backups are encrypted and restore-tested.

21. Genealogy and cultural-rule engineering
Store persons, parent-child edges, spouse/union history, aliases, sources, claims, disputes and effective status as separately governed records.
Validate impossible cycles, conflicting parentage, date inconsistencies, duplicates and self-relationships before approval.
Merges preserve canonical identity, all source references, prior identifiers, decisions and rollback/compensation evidence.
Nata/Saino returns primary and alternative valid paths under versioned priority rules; unsupported terminology uses neutral path wording.
Adopted, step, half, guardian, former-spouse, unknown and disputed states remain explicit and privacy-aware.
Jutho, Tithi and Shraddha calculators activate only with approved versioned rule tables, timezone/calendar source and reference tests.
Every calculated cultural output displays rule version, explanation, authority status and correction route.

32. Rollback and forward-fix rules
Rollback triggers are defined before release and include security/privacy exposure, data corruption, severe availability loss and failed critical journeys.
Schema changes use expand/migrate/contract or another approved backward-compatible method; irreversible operations require explicit executive risk acceptance.
Feature flags can disable risky behaviour without removing audit or data integrity.
Rollback never discards accepted member data; reconciliation and compensating actions are documented.
Any emergency forward-fix receives expedited independent review and complete after-action evidence.

34. Agency contract and handover controls
All work products, source code, designs, documentation, configurations and deliverables are assigned to Jyphra under the signed contract.
Agency access is named, least-privilege, MFA-protected, time-bound and revoked promptly at role end.
No supplier-owned repository, cloud account, signing key or undocumented paid dependency may become a production prerequisite.
Invoices/funding milestones align to accepted gate evidence, not effort claims alone.
Handover includes architecture, source/build, environments, secrets custody, data models, APIs, tests, monitoring, incidents, known risks and operational training.
Warranty terms define severity response, defect correction, exclusions and transition support.
Exit assistance and replaceability are tested before final payment.
