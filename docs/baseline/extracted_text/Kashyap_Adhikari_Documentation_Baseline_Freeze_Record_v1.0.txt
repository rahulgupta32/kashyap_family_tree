1. Freeze decision
Jyphra Technology Pvt. Ltd. establishes this package as the authoritative Development Baseline v1.0 for the Kashyap Adhikari Genealogy & Community Platform. The documented Release 1.0 scope is frozen. Engineering, design, procurement, data preparation, testing and agency delivery must trace to this baseline. No feature, rule, control or quality target may be removed, deferred or materially altered without an approved change request.
The freeze locks scope and approved planning assumptions. It does not convert pending cultural, legal, personnel, procurement, test or store validations into completed approvals.
2. Authority and ownership
Jyphra Technology Pvt. Ltd. owns the product, documentation, source code, design assets, repositories, cloud accounts, credentials, data-control decisions and intellectual property.
Jyphra is the final business acceptance and release authority.
An external development agency may execute contracted work only within the Jyphra-approved baseline, repositories, security controls, quality gates and acceptance process.
Community, cultural, genealogy, privacy and security authorities retain the specialist approval powers defined in the governance framework.
No supplier, developer or individual administrator may unilaterally amend the baseline.

5. Frozen decisions
All documented features are included in Release 1.0; there is no approved feature deferral.
Target platforms include Android, iOS and the web administration portal, with Nepali and English localization.
Architecture must support at least 100,000 registered users at launch and growth toward 500,000 without core redesign.
Planning baseline: 20,000 MAU, 5,000 DAU and approximately 1,000 normal-peak concurrent users.
Delivery period: 12–15 months.
Delivery: Jyphra-led hybrid team with external development-agency capacity managed by Jyphra.
Support: business-hours monitoring and user support, with an emergency contact route outside business hours and no 24/7 user-support SLA.
Cloud: managed critical services combined with cost-efficient compute/containers.
Minimum availability objective: 99.5%, subject to the detailed SLOs in the Master Specification.
Financial planning: NPR, individual compensation undisclosed, 15% implementation contingency, taxes separate, three-year TCO, no revenue model.
Recommended Production-Grade scenario is the approved financial planning baseline.

8. Change-control procedure
Step
Mandatory control
1. Raise
Assign a unique change ID and state reason, affected requirement IDs, requested outcome and proposer.
2. Analyse
Document product, cultural, privacy, security, data, cost, schedule, testing and operational impact.
3. Review
Obtain domain-owner reviews and agency estimate where applicable.
4. Decide
Jyphra Product Owner approves/rejects; high-risk, cultural, legal, security or budget changes require the defined specialist authority.
5. Version
Create a new controlled version; never silently overwrite frozen history.
6. Trace
Update affected requirements, backlog, tests, designs, APIs, data rules, budget and release notes.
7. Communicate
Notify delivery, governance and operational stakeholders before implementation.
8. Verify
Confirm implementation and acceptance evidence against the approved change.
9. Baseline integrity and storage
SHA-256 checksums identify the exact bytes included in the freeze package.
The ZIP archive is a transport copy; the manifest and controlled source files remain the authority.
Working files, render outputs, superseded drafts and training examples are excluded unless explicitly registered.
A changed checksum requires a new controlled version and updated manifest.
Jyphra must store the baseline in a restricted, backed-up repository with read access for the delivery team and write access limited to authorized document controllers.
