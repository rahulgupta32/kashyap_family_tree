SDLC scope
1. Product Vision
1.1 Problem Statement
The platform will provide a trusted, centralized and continuously maintained digital system for preserving and managing Kashyap/Kashyapa Gotra Adhikari genealogy, history, culture and community activities across Nepal. The verified genealogy is the system of record on which relationship discovery, family events, notifications, invitations, Jutho/Shraddha services, community interactions and communication depend.
The product must solve the following problems:
Genealogy information is distributed across generations, branches, locations and historical records and must be preserved in a structured, searchable form.
Members need a controlled way to contribute corrections and additions without directly compromising the official genealogy.
Direct family facts must be verified and indirect relationships calculated automatically from the trusted graph.
Cultural and religious knowledge must be managed as authoritative, versioned content under designated governance.
Community events, invitations, announcements and communication must be connected to the verified family network.
The application must be attractive and easy to understand at first use while remaining fast, bilingual, accessible and dependable.
National production use requires security, privacy, moderation, auditability, backup, disaster recovery and operational support from day one.
1.2 Product Value Proposition
A verified digital genealogy and cultural network for Kashyap Gotra Adhikari families in which identity, family relationships, events, invitations, community communication and culturally governed services operate around one trusted family graph.
1.3 Long-Term Vision
Establish the authoritative, community-governed digital genealogy and cultural archive of Kashyap/Kashyapa Gotra Adhikari families across Nepal, capable of preserving many generations of history while giving living members a modern, secure and culturally respectful community platform.
1.4 Business Objectives
Preserve historical genealogy and cultural knowledge for present and future generations.
Create one verified and searchable family network across branches and locations.
Allow controlled member contributions while protecting official genealogy integrity.
Automatically identify family relationships and approved Nepali Nata/Saino terminology.
Improve awareness of birthdays, deaths, Shraddha, 84 Puja, marriage, Bratabandha, Dewali/Goth Puja, gatherings and community events.
Provide digital invitations with genealogy- and relationship-aware recipient selection.
Enable community announcements, missing-person notices, property/room notices, assistance requests and programs with responsible moderation.
Provide one-to-one and group communication with media sharing, read state, blocking, reporting and safety controls.
Maintain transparent administration, privacy controls, auditability, backup and disaster recovery.
Deliver a polished bilingual application that represents the community professionally in the market.

3. Core Domain Principle: Person Is Not User Account
APersonis a permanent genealogy entity. A Person may be a registered member, living non-user, child, deceased person or historical ancestor. AUser Accountis an authentication identity that can use application features. These concepts remain separate throughout the data model, APIs and lifecycle.
Consequences:
Every Person receives a permanent unique Person ID.
Historical ancestors and non-users can exist without an account.
A User Account gains association with a Person only after an approved profile claim or authorized administrative action.
Deleting/deactivating an account does not automatically erase historical genealogy facts.
Genealogy permissions depend on verified Person linkage, role, branch scope and privacy rules.

14.1 Supported Languages

14.2 Terminology Governance
Canonical product terms such as Person, User Account, Branch, Claim, Genealogy Change Request, Verifier, Nata/Saino, Jutho, Tithi and Shraddha must have an approved glossary.
Cultural/religious terms should preserve the approved Nepali term and use explanatory English translations rather than forcing misleading literal substitutions.

Error messages must explain what happened and what the user can do next; internal stack traces/codes are never shown as the only user message.

Persist original source calendar/value and normalized machine-comparable representations where safe.

Do not imply precision for incomplete historical dates; support year-only/month-only/unknown components where the data model allows.
All conversions must identify the approved conversion/rule implementation version where domain correctness depends on it.
14.4 Tone
Member-facing language shall be respectful, clear and non-judgmental. Genealogy disagreements, death records, Jutho and religious guidance require especially neutral wording. Administrative decision copy shall separate factual validation status from personal judgment.

17. Data Governance Specification

Person identity/genealogy:canonical relational database after approved import/change.

Cultural content:latest published approved content revision.

Account/security state:identity service/database records.
Media binary:private object storage; metadata and authorization in application database.

17.2 Data Quality Rules
Never create a new Person without duplicate-candidate evaluation.
Preserve source/provenance for imported historical data whenever available.
Support incomplete historical fields without inventing missing values.
Corrections retain before/after history.
Conflicting sources are represented as review work, not automatically resolved by arbitrary precedence.
Imported or member-contributed facts have verification state and source metadata.
A designated data steward owns import reconciliation and quality reports.
17.3 Correction and Dispute Handling
Identify target Person/fact.
Capture proposed correction and evidence/source.
Determine authority scope.
Review graph impact and duplicates.
Approve/reject/request correction/escalate.
Apply approved mutation atomically.
Invalidate derived data/caches.
Notify relevant parties where policy allows.
Preserve audit and provenance.

18.1 Core Data Types and Conventions
IDs: UUID/UUIDv7 or equivalent globally unique, non-sequential public-safe identifiers; final choice recorded by ADR.
Timestamps: timezone-aware UTC in storage; localized on presentation.
Soft deletion:archived_at/deleted_atonly where domain permits; genealogy/audit history follows stricter governance.
Money: not currently a core domain; no floating-point monetary storage if introduced later by approved scope change.
Phone: normalized E.164 representation plus verified flag; display formatting localized.
Names: Unicode text with original script and normalized search keys separated.
Geographic data: suitable spatial/geography type when selected DB extension is approved.
JSON: permitted for bounded configuration/snapshots, not as a substitute for relational integrity in core genealogy.
19. Genealogy Import and Migration Plan
The system is expected to ingest existing genealogy information. Import must be treated as a controlled data-engineering program rather than a one-time spreadsheet upload.
Source inventory:identify books, Word/PDF tables, spreadsheets, legacy databases and branch-maintained records.
Source ownership:assign branch/contact and provenance to every dataset.
Canonical mapping:map source fields into Person, names, direct relationships, branch, dates, location and source references.
Cleansing:normalize whitespace, phone/date formats and obvious encoding issues without changing historical meaning.
Identity matching:detect exact and probabilistic Person candidates using name/family/date/branch/location evidence.
Relationship validation:check self-links, cycles, implausible parentage/date inconsistencies and disconnected components.
Dry run:produce counts for inserted, matched, duplicate-candidate, invalid and unresolved records; no production mutation.
Human reconciliation:data steward/verifiers resolve blocked cases and document decisions.
Commit:import approved batch atomically or in controlled resumable chunks with immutable batch ID.
Post-import reconciliation:compare source counts, Person/link counts, duplicate results and exception reports.
Rollback/repair:retain batch provenance and migration scripts so a faulty batch can be reversed/corrected under governance.
Every import produces anImport Audit Reportcontaining source, checksum, mapping version, dry-run results, approver, commit time, counts, failures and reconciliation result.

20.4 Architecture Principles
Modular monolith first, clear module boundaries always.One deployable API can reduce operational complexity while domain modules remain separable; scaling pressure may split services without changing contracts.
Relational truth for genealogy.PostgreSQL constraints/transactions provide durable governance; graph traversal is implemented with recursive SQL/optimized indexes/caches unless measurement proves a dedicated graph database necessary.
Rule-driven cultural logic.Nata/Saino, Jutho and Tithi rules are versioned data/configuration with test cases and approval, not scattered conditional code.
Zero trust of client permissions.Mobile/web UI hiding never substitutes for server authorization.
Asynchronous side effects.Notifications, media transforms and non-critical integrations occur after durable transaction commit using reliable jobs/outbox patterns.
Observable by default.Every request, job and external integration has correlation IDs, structured logs and metrics.
Privacy by design.Only necessary fields are returned, location is minimized, evidence is restricted, and admin power is bounded/audited.

23. API Engineering Contract
23.1 API Conventions
Base path:/api/v1.
JSON over HTTPS for REST APIs; WebSocket for real-time communication.
OpenAPI 3.x is the executable contract for REST endpoints.
Authentication: bearer access token with short lifetime; secure refresh/session rotation; privileged web sessions follow stronger policy.
Authorization: endpoint + action + resource + branch/relationship/ownership policy; never trust role claims without server verification.
Dates/times: ISO-8601 UTC timestamps plus explicit domain date fields for BS/Tithi source representations.
Pagination: cursor-based for feeds/messages/audit where stable ordering is required; bounded page size.
Filtering/sorting: explicit allowlists; no arbitrary client-provided SQL field exposure.
Idempotency: required for retriable creates such as invitations, messages, uploads and sensitive mutations.
Versioning: breaking API changes require a new API version or backward-compatible migration plan.
Validation errors: stable machine code + localized user-safe message key/details.
Correlation: every response includes or makes available a request/correlation identifier.

25. Key Sequence Specifications
25.1 OTP Authentication
Client submits normalized phone number.
API applies device/IP/account-abuse limits.
API creates short-lived OTP challenge and requests SMS through provider adapter.
Client submits code + challenge ID.
Server validates attempts, expiry, one-time use and phone binding.
Server creates/activates UserAccount if allowed and issues session tokens.
Device/push token registration occurs separately.
Audit/security telemetry records outcome without logging OTP.
25.2 Profile Claim Approval
Member searches existing People.
Member selects Person and submits claim/evidence.
System performs conflict/duplicate checks and assigns verifier scope.
Verifier opens claim with permitted genealogy context.
Verifier approves, rejects, requests correction or escalates.
On approval, transaction links UserAccount to Person according to uniqueness policy.
Audit event and notification are generated after commit.
Member session refresh exposes claimed-person capabilities.
25.3 Genealogy Change Approval
Member prepares proposal against canonical Person/link version.
API validates input, authority to request and duplicate candidates.
Request stores immutable before/proposed snapshots and evidence references.
Verifier reviews graph-impact preview.
Decision is permission/state validated.
Approved mutation executes atomically with optimistic concurrency/version check.
Derived relationship caches/search projections are invalidated/rebuilt.
Audit/outbox event is committed transactionally.
Notifications and downstream jobs run asynchronously.
25.4 Duplicate Merge
System/admin identifies candidate Person records.
Merge console displays names, direct relationships, sources, claims, events and conflicts.
Admin selects canonical values and resolves every blocking conflict.
System simulates resulting graph and prevents invalid cycles/duplicate links.
Authorized confirmation executes single merge transaction.
References move to canonical Person; source Person IDs become merge-history mappings.
Search/cache projections update.
Audit captures complete merge plan/result.
25.5 Invitation with Genealogy Audience
Organizer creates event/invitation.
Organizer selects individual, branch, generation, descendants or relationship-based audience.
Relationship service resolves permitted recipients from verified graph.
UI shows recipient preview/count; organizer confirms.
Server persists recipient snapshot and invitation version.
Delivery jobs create notifications.
Invitees RSVP; organizer summary updates.
Later genealogy changes do not silently rewrite already-sent recipient history; explicit invitation update is required.
25.6 Message and Media Upload
Client requests media upload authorization.
API checks conversation membership and file policy.
Client uploads directly to private object storage using short-lived authorization.
Completion triggers security validation/scanning/derivative processing.
When media is ready, client sends idempotent message referencing media asset.
Server commits message/sequence and publishes real-time event.
Offline recipient receives push notification according to privacy preference.
Delivery/read receipts update asynchronously.

26. Security Requirements Specification
26.1 Authentication and Session Management
OTP challenge is short-lived, single-use and attempt-limited.
OTP secrets are stored only in a secure verifier form and never plaintext logs.
Access tokens are short-lived; refresh/session credentials rotate and can be revoked per device or globally.
Admin authentication uses stronger policy than member access; production architecture shall support MFA/step-up for privileged or destructive operations.
Session issuance records device/session metadata needed for security review without covert device fingerprinting beyond policy.
Authentication responses prevent practical account enumeration.
26.2 Authorization
Server-side RBAC combined with branch scope, resource ownership, relationship/visibility rules and workflow state.
Deny by default for privileged actions.
Every admin endpoint has explicit required permission(s).
Verifier cannot approve their own restricted/conflicting request where governance policy prohibits conflict of interest.
Super Admin privileges are minimized, monitored and reviewed.
Background workers use service identities with least privilege.
26.3 Data Protection
TLS in transit; managed encryption at rest for DB/object storage/backups.
Secrets and encryption keys are stored/rotated via managed secret/key services.
Restricted evidence and private media use private object storage and short-lived access.
Sensitive fields are excluded/masked from logs and analytics.
Production data is not copied to developer/test environments without approved anonymization/sanitization.
26.4 Input, File and API Security
Central request schema validation and output serialization.
Parameterized database access/ORM/query builder; no string-built SQL from user input.
File extension, MIME, signature, size and malware/content checks.
Image processing uses isolated/updated libraries with resource limits.
URL inputs used by integrations are allowlisted/validated against SSRF.
Rate limiting and abuse detection for OTP, login, search, chat, posting, invitations and admin actions.
CORS/CSRF/security headers configured for admin web architecture.
26.5 Audit and Security Monitoring
Security-significant events include failed admin login, role changes, session revocations, repeated OTP abuse, unauthorized attempts, mass export, merge, rule activation, import commit, moderation override and configuration changes. Alerts are based on risk/volume rather than logging every benign read at high severity.

40.4 Network and Security Boundaries
Public mobile/admin traffic terminates at a managed edge/load-balancing layer with TLS and web/API protections.
Application services live in controlled private network segments wherever cloud topology permits.
PostgreSQL and Redis are not internet-accessible.
Administrative database access occurs only through audited operational mechanisms; application admins do not receive direct DB credentials.

Outbound integrations are limited to approved endpoints and secrets.
Production observability systems receive sanitized telemetry, not unrestricted raw personal data.
41. Observability Specification
41.1 Logs
Structured production logs contain timestamp, service, environment, severity, request/job ID, safe actor/entity identifiers where justified, operation name, latency/result and error code. Logs must redact tokens, OTPs, evidence content, raw message bodies unless a tightly controlled diagnostic policy explicitly requires otherwise, and unnecessary profile fields.
41.2 Metrics
Minimum service metrics:
request count, latency and error rate by endpoint/module;
OTP request/verify/delivery success and throttling rate;
active sessions and authentication failure anomalies;
DB connections, CPU, storage, slow queries and replication/backup status;
Redis health and cache/lock behavior;
worker queue depth, age, retries and dead letters;
push/SMS/maps/calendar provider latency/failure rate;
upload processing/scan failures;
search latency and zero-result rate;
relationship/Jutho/Tithi unmapped/unavailable rate;
chat active connections and message delivery latency;
moderation queue size/age;
import validation/reconciliation errors;
crash-free mobile sessions and app startup failures.
41.3 Tracing and Correlation
Distributed/cross-service operations shall propagate correlation identifiers across API, worker, realtime and external-adapter calls. A support engineer should be able to start from a user-visiblerequestIdand find the corresponding sanitized operational trace.
41.4 Alerts
Alert conditions include sustained high API errors/latency, database saturation, queue lag, failed backups, failed restore test, authentication attack spikes, external-provider outage, storage capacity risk, message delivery degradation and unhandled critical application exceptions. Alerts map to an owner and runbook.

45. Security and Privacy Operations
Privileged roles are reviewed regularly and removed promptly when no longer required.
Production access uses named accounts; shared administrator credentials are prohibited.
Break-glass access is documented, tightly restricted and audited.
Secrets/keys rotate according to provider/security policy and immediately after compromise suspicion.
Vulnerability remediation uses risk-based SLA; Critical/High exploitable issues block release.
Data subject/member requests are tracked with identity verification and documented completion.
Security/privacy incidents have a legal/escalation path appropriate to Nepal and any applicable hosting/provider jurisdictions.
46. Data Retention and Archival Principles
Exact legal retention periods require counsel/business approval; engineering shall implement configurable retention policies rather than hard-code arbitrary periods. Baseline principles:
canonical genealogy facts may require long-term preservation as community historical records, subject to lawful/privacy governance;
closed UserAccount authentication/session data is retained only as necessary for security/legal/business obligations;
OTP verification artifacts are short-lived;
evidence and moderation records follow a justified restricted retention schedule;
messages/community media follow documented user-facing retention/deletion policy;
audit records retain sufficient history for governance/security and are archived securely;
backup retention does not become a hidden permanent archive after primary deletion policy expires;
legal holds override normal deletion only through documented authority.

Appendix B — Detailed Use Cases and User Journeys
The following use cases provide the actor/precondition/trigger/normal/alternative/failure/postcondition/notification structure required for implementation and QA.
UC-01 Register and Verify Mobile Number
Actor:MemberPreconditions:App installed; network available; number not prohibited.Trigger:User chooses to register/sign in.Normal flow:select language -> enter phone -> request OTP -> receive code -> enter code -> server verifies -> create/restore UserAccount -> issue secure session -> proceed to profile discovery/home according to account state.Alternative flows:resend after cooldown; already-registered account resumes; device notification permission deferred until contextual prompt.Failure cases:invalid number, provider failure, expired/incorrect OTP, rate limit, suspended account.Postcondition:authenticated valid session or no session.Notifications:OTP SMS; no sensitive push needed.Acceptance:AUTH-FR-* and NFR security targets pass.
UC-02 Claim Existing Genealogy Profile
Actor:Member, Verifier, Super Admin.Preconditions:Authenticated UserAccount; searchable genealogy exists.Trigger:Member identifies a Person representing themselves.Normal flow:open Person candidate -> review context -> attach required family/evidence -> submit -> scope assign -> verifier reviews -> approves -> UserAccount links to Person -> notify member.Alternative:verifier requests correction; member resubmits; verifier escalates conflict.Failure:conflicting claim, insufficient authority, invalid evidence, Person merged during review.Postcondition:approved unique account-person link or closed/non-approved claim with complete history.Notifications:submission acknowledgment, correction/rejection/approval.Acceptance:CLAIM-FR-001..012, AUD-FR-*.
UC-03 Request New Person and Prevent Duplicate
Actor:Member, System, Verifier/Admin.Preconditions:Member did not find correct Person.Trigger:Member chooses“request new Person.”Normal flow:enter identity + parent/spouse/branch context -> system searches candidates -> member/verifier resolves candidate -> if truly new, submit -> verifier approves -> Person created and optionally claim linked.Alternative:candidate is existing Person -> route to claim/correction rather than create.Failure:insufficient identifying context, invalid family link, duplicate conflict.Postcondition:one canonical Person, never an unmanaged duplicate.Acceptance:DUP-FR-, GEN-FR-.
UC-04 Add or Correct Genealogy
Actor:Member, Verifier, Super Admin.Preconditions:Authenticated member; target visible.Trigger:Add parent/spouse/child/relative, correct data/link, or record death.Normal flow:select change -> capture proposed facts/evidence -> submit -> reviewer sees before/after and graph impact -> approve -> atomic mutation -> relationship/search caches refresh -> notify.Alternative:correction requested or escalation.Failure:duplicate candidate, invalid cycle, stale version conflict, out-of-scope reviewer.Postcondition:trusted graph changes only after successful approved commit.Acceptance:CHG-FR-*, BR-GEN-003.
UC-05 Discover Relationship / Nata-Saino
Actor:Member.Preconditions:Viewer linked to a Person or selects two permitted People; verified path exists.Trigger:Opens Person/relationship explorer.Normal flow:relationship service traverses verified graph -> determines genealogical relationship -> active approved rule maps Nepali term -> response includes English term and safe path.Alternative:path exists but cultural mapping not approved -> neutral relationship path shown.Failure:no verified path or traversal limit.Postcondition:no genealogy mutation.Acceptance:REL-FR-* and signed reference cases.
UC-06 Determine Jutho After Approved Death
Actor:Member/System/Religious Authority.Preconditions:death fact approved; viewer/selected Person and deceased have verified relationship path; approved Jutho rule set effective.Trigger:Member opens relevant death/Jutho view or system generates a permitted reminder.Normal flow:resolve path -> apply rule/exceptions -> return approved duration/indication and explanatory content -> store rule version for material notification/audit.Alternative:multiple paths resolved by approved precedence.Failure:incomplete/unverified genealogy or no active authoritative mapping -> clearly unavailable, never guessed.Acceptance:JUT-FR-* and signed rule suite.
UC-07 Create Event and Genealogy-Aware Invitation
Actor:Authorized Member/Admin, Invitee.Preconditions:organizer active and event authority valid.Trigger:organizer creates invitation.Normal flow:create/select event -> enter details/date/venue -> choose individuals/branch/generation/descendants/relationship group -> system resolves recipient preview -> organizer confirms -> persist snapshot -> notify -> invitees RSVP -> organizer sees summary.Alternative:update/cancel invitation with new notification.Failure:invalid audience, privacy restriction, excessively large/rate-limited send, calendar source unavailable.Postcondition:immutable sent-recipient snapshot plus current RSVP state.Acceptance:INV-FR-, CAL-FR-.
UC-08 Publish and Moderate Community Post
Actor:Member, Moderator.Preconditions:verified eligible account.Trigger:create post in supported category.Normal flow:compose -> validate media/location/contact -> category policy decides immediate publication or pre-review -> feed visibility -> user report if needed -> moderator investigates -> keep/restrict/remove/escalate -> notify/audit.Failure:unsafe media, prohibited content/category, rate limit, privacy violation.Postcondition:post/revisions/moderation history consistent with policy.Acceptance:COM-FR-*.
UC-09 Use Household/Community Map Safely
Actor:Member.Preconditions:authenticated and eligible to view map.Trigger:open map/search branch.Normal flow:API evaluates location visibility -> returns permitted precision -> map renders markers -> member filters/opens safe household/community details.Failure:no location permission required for general browse; map provider outage uses textual/limited fallback.Postcondition:no private precise location disclosure.Acceptance:MAP-FR-* and privacy tests.
UC-10 Direct and Group Messaging
Actor:Member.Preconditions:authenticated eligible participants; no blocking restriction.Trigger:start/open direct or group conversation.Normal flow:authorize membership -> load paginated history -> send idempotent text/media -> commit -> real-time event -> push if offline -> receipts update.Alternative:offline sender queues and retries; group admin changes membership.Failure:blocked user, removed participant, unsafe media, rate limit.Postcondition:message history/receipts follow retention and privacy rules.Acceptance:CHAT-FR-*.
UC-11 Publish Cultural Content / Domain Rules
Actor:Content Manager, Religious Representative, Super Admin governance.Preconditions:authorized role and source/provenance available.Trigger:new/revised article or rule set.Normal flow:draft -> add translation/references/tests -> submit review -> designated authority approves -> publish/effect -> member/cache/search update -> old version retained.Failure:failed reference tests, missing translation/provenance, unauthorized approver.Postcondition:only approved effective version drives authoritative behavior.Acceptance:CUL-FR-*, REL-FR-013, JUT-FR-002/003, CAL-FR-006/007.
UC-12 Import Genealogy
Actor:Data Steward, Super Admin.Preconditions:source inventoried, mapped, checksum/provenance captured.Trigger:create import batch.Normal flow:upload -> map -> validate -> duplicate/cycle checks -> dry-run report -> human reconciliation -> approval -> commit -> post-import reconciliation -> archive report.Alternative:blocked batch repaired and re-dry-run.Failure:schema mismatch, excessive unresolved duplicates, broken relationship integrity, stale source.Postcondition:committed batch is reproducible/auditable; failures do not partially corrupt trusted graph.Acceptance:GEN/DUP/AUD requirements and migration test suite.
