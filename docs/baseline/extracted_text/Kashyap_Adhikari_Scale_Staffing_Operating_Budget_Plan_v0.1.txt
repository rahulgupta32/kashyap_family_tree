2. Binding planning assumptions
Capacity for at least 100,000 registered users at launch, with a path to 500,000 without redesigning the core architecture.
Baseline demand: 20,000 monthly active users, 5,000 daily active users and approximately 1,000 normal-peak concurrent users.
Twelve-to-fifteen-month full Release 1.0 delivery; no documented product feature is intentionally deferred.
Jyphra owns the product, approvals, source repositories, cloud accounts, credentials, intellectual property and acceptance decisions.
A contracted external development agency may supply implementation capacity, but remains governed and accepted by Jyphra.
Business-hours monitoring and user support; severe incidents use an emergency contact path outside hours without a guaranteed 24/7 SLA.
Hybrid cloud: managed services for critical data/identity/storage/backup components and cost-efficient compute or containers for suitable services.
A 15% implementation contingency is held separately. Three-year operating forecasts exclude a second contingency to avoid double counting; Jyphra should reforecast quarterly.
No revenue, subscription, advertising or sponsorship assumptions are included.

7. Scenario interpretation
7.1 Minimum professional
Suitable only if funding is constrained and Jyphra accepts slower delivery, smaller device coverage, limited parallelism, narrower independent assurance and greater dependency on carefully controlled rollout. It must not reduce privacy, audit, backup or core security controls.
7.2 Recommended production-grade
The approved planning baseline. It provides sufficient delivery capacity for the full Release 1.0 scope, structured migration, automated testing, independent security work, multi-environment deployment and sustainable business-hours operations for the initial 100,000-user target.
7.3 Enterprise strengthened
Adds delivery parallelism, stronger high-availability engineering, broader device and performance assurance, deeper operational coverage and additional data/community operations. Select this scenario if launch commitments, geographic rollout or public visibility make reduced delivery risk more valuable than the additional cost.

10. Acceptance and approval conditions
Jyphra approves one scenario as the active funding baseline and records its budget authority.
Finance and procurement confirm VAT, withholding, payment currency and supplier terms before signing.
Technology leadership converts the selected scenario into a bill of quantities and live vendor quotations.
The agency contract includes intellectual-property assignment, confidentiality, privacy/security duties, acceptance criteria, warranty, handover and exit assistance.
Release funding is stopped or rebaselined if gate evidence fails, material scope changes, or forecast variance exceeds the approved tolerance.
Operating budget ownership is assigned before production and includes backup restore tests, incident handling, patching, moderation and support.
